# DSPY ZONE Photo Assets

This redesign is structured for real DSPY ZONE photography.

Replace the temporary stock image URLs in `style.css` with approved DSPY ZONE photos before presenting the redesign as a final client concept.

Recommended photo set:

1. Hero, wide training floor or strongest exterior/interior image.
2. Strength, weights and machines.
3. Cardio, treadmill or conditioning area.
4. Personal training, coach working with a member.
5. Ladies fitness, approved women’s training image from DSPY ZONE.
6. Weight loss, training session.
7. Sport massage, approved recovery image.
8. Gallery, three or more real gym interior photos.
9. Team, one approved portrait for each listed team member.
10. CTA, strongest DSPY ZONE training image.

Use only photos supplied by DSPY ZONE or photos they have explicitly approved for website use. Public directory images should not be treated as approved brand assets.
